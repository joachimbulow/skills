# High-Converting Webshop Builder

A specialized skill for building interactive, production-ready, high-converting e-commerce web applications and DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) storefronts in HTML/CSS/JS or React.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- The user requests a high-converting webshop, landing page, PDP (Product Detail Page), or DTC storefront.
- The goal is to maximize conversion rates, reduce friction, and boost average order value (AOV).
- Building an interactive prototype or single-page webshop with integrated checkout flows, sticky CTA bars, variant switchers, and social proof elements.

## Essential CRO Architecture & Design Principles

Every webshop generated by this skill must incorporate proven conversion rate optimization (CRO) patterns:

### 1. Above-the-Fold (ATF) Conversion Drivers
- **Value Proposition Headline:** Clear, benefit-focused messaging (what it is + key benefit).
- **Social Proof & Ratings:** Aggregate review score (e.g., "★ 4.9/5 from 3,400+ reviews") placed right near the headline or Buy CTA.
- **Micro-Trust Badges:** 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, Free Shipping threshold, Secure Checkout icons.
- **Primary CTA:** High-contrast, prominent "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" button paired with express checkout options (Apple Pay / Google Pay / Shop Pay).

### 2. Friction Reduction & Checkout UX
- **Sticky Converting Bar:** Appears when scrolling past the main hero section to ensure the primary CTA is always visible.
- **Low-Friction Checkout Modal:** Multi-step or 1-click guest checkout modal with inline validation, order bump options, and auto-calculated total savings.
- **Clear Price Hierarchy:** Show original price crossed out alongside discounted current price and explicit savings badges (e.g., "Save $50 Today").

### 3. Interactive Product Studio & Media
- **Dynamic Variant Switcher:** Instant color/size/model selection updating product previews, stock indicators, and pricing dynamically.
- **Value Feature Badges:** Grid displaying key differentiators with visual icons.

### 4. Psychological & Behavioral Triggers
- **Real-Time Urgency & Scarcity:** Low-stock indicators (e.g., "Only 14 left in stock for fast delivery") and limited-time offer countdowns.
- **Customer Reviews & Social Proof:** Filterable reviews with verified purchaser badges, customer photos/stars, and real-time purchase notification popups.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Addressing top pre-purchase objections (returns, shipping, compatibility, warranty).

## Implementation Workflow

When asked to generate a high-converting webshop:

1. **Extract Context & Product Details:**
   - Identify product category, key features, pricing, target persona, and color theme from conversation context.
   - If details are unspecified, choose a premium, compelling DTC product niche (e.g., premium audio, high-performance wearables, smart home devices, or artisan coffee gear).

2. **Structure the Application UI:**
   - **Header:** Announcement bar (e.g., "FREE EXPRESS SHIPPING OVER $50 • 30-DAY RISK-FREE TRIAL"), logo, search, cart trigger with counter badge.
   - **Hero PDP:** Interactive gallery, variant selector, quantity selector, primary CTA, express payment badges, trust guarantees.
   - **Sticky Bottom/Top Bar:** Compact bar with thumbnail, selected variant, price, and instant Buy CTA.
   - **Social Proof Ticker:** Customer press logos, trust badges, star ratings.
   - **Feature Showcase:** Interactive tabbed grid highlighting core product benefits.
   - **Reviews Section:** Verified customer reviews with star ratings, filter buttons, and summary stats.
   - **FAQ Accordion:** Objections and answers expandable UI.
   - **Express Checkout Modal:** Multi-step cart & checkout experience with promo code support, order bump upsell, and payment option tabs.

3. **Output Execution:**
   - Generate full, clean, standalone HTML/CSS/JS (or React code via web_code_canvas) that renders seamlessly with high visual aesthetic, modern typographic hierarchy, responsive layouts, and smooth animations.
